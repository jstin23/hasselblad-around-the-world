What Hasselblad gets

Twelve packages, for Hasselblad's channels only.

Twelve reel packages, three from each of four winter locations, shot to the Hasselblad Content Reference.

In every package

  • 1 main-subject BTS clip, 4K 9:16, whole camera and rear screen in frame, full process to the shutter
  • 3 finished 100MP stills from the location
  • 1 screen-recording of the frame being reviewed on the camera
  • One folder per package, named to your checklist

When it lands

  • Dec 5 package one, for your review
  • Dec 12 Japan, three packages
  • Dec 22 New Zealand, three packages
  • Feb 15 Canada and USA, six packages
  • Twelve complete, one folder each, by February 15

Built for reception

  • Four locations across two hemispheres, so no two packages look like the same trip
  • Hasselblad posts first, tagged and credited
  • Once posted, Justin shares each reel to his 581K as a collaborator, so the views land on your account
Production support Each location is a multi-day shoot: travel, permits, boats and helicopters to reach the spots that rarely get photographed, and post-production. The kit covers the content. A production support contribution alongside it covers getting the camera to those places, and it all goes back into what lands in your folder. Justin is happy to scope that with you, or with whoever on the marketing side holds that budget.
